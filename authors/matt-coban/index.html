Matt Coban

Matt Coban is a Research Technologist and an Employee Professional Master’s student in the BMB track. Matt has been working at Mayo since 2016, engaging in structural biology, biochemistry and cell culture-based investigations of serine proteases and their inhibitors. Before that he was in the Navy as an electronics technician, specializing in equipment involved in ballistic missile defense. He enjoys learning how everything in the universe works, writing (but not finishing) absurd science fiction novels, and taking things apart.
