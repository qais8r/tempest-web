A voice in The Tempest

Jeesoo Yoon

Jeesoo Yoon is a first-year medical student in Rochester, MN. She was born in Seoul, South Korea, but calls Seattle home. In her free time, she plays the cello, practices film photography, grows cash crops on her balcony, watches too many videos of rats, and scream-sings ABBA in the passenger seat. Her writing is interested in the rituals that people build around ordinary life. Her biggest regret in life is that she is fatally allergic to cats and dogs.
